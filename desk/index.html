Method. Weekly taste levels from Qloo /v2/trending (log change in population_percentile), one affinity-weighted basket per sub-sector from Qloo /v2/insights, every name in the book excluded from every basket. Per holding: OLS of the aligned weekly returns on SPY and its sub-sector's basket. The basket coefficient is the cultural beta; standard errors in grey; ΔR² is the gain over the market-only fit.
